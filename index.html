02 Homepage Rewrite

The current hero misses the business outcome.

SagePilot's current messaging leads with "AI Employees." Memorable, but a founder landing cold doesn't yet understand what that actually replaces or saves. The rewrite leads with the outcome first, proves the capability second.

Current
We build AI Employees
Autonomous coworkers that onboard into your team and deliver exceptional experiences across your entire customer lifecycle.
Problem: A founder still has to imagine what this means for their business. What's the business outcome? What do I replace? What do I save?
Recommended
Turn customer touchpoints into revenue touchpoints.
Every interaction from first click to loyalty moment is a chance to acquire, convert, or retain. SagePilot's AI employees manage all of it, on-brand, at scale, without hiring proportionally.
Why: Opens with the business problem (customer touchpoints = revenue moments). Then explains what's different (on-brand, at scale, without headcount). Makes the value immediate.

Expected impact: Hypothesis: this messaging should increase homepage-to-demo conversion by clarifying what the business outcome actually is (revenue per lifecycle stage, not just "AI technology"). I'd validate with an A/B test before rolling out to paid traffic.

03 Sales Battlecard

Sales wins when they can respond in 10 seconds.

Most of SagePilot's losses will come from one objection: "This looks like a chatbot." Here's the battlecard sales needs before the first call.

Objection
Isn't this just a chatbot?
Why they say it
Chatbots are everywhere. They're cheap. They fail spectacularly. Buyer has been burned by one. SagePilot looks like "another AI" until proven otherwise.
Your response
Not if it understands your brand. Chatbots give generic responses. SagePilot learns your brand voice, your product strategy, your customer journey. It automates every stage of customer lifecycle, not just customer service. That's the difference between responding to customers and advancing them toward purchase.
Proof point
Pull a real customer case study from SagePilot's customer list (from their D2C, retail, or beauty verticals). Show the actual before/after metrics on acquisition, conversion, or support efficiency. The conversation shifts immediately from "is this just a chatbot?" to "what specifically improved for them?"
Next action
Demo the lifecycle view. Show how the same AI employee moves a customer from discovery to purchase to retention. End with: "What does your customer journey look like today?" (Reframe from product to their problem.)

Expected impact: Sales team tests this response in the next 20 calls. Track win rate on deals where this objection appears. If win rate goes from 22% to 31%, expand the battlecard playbook across all common objections.

05 Execution Plan

Not what to do. What to do first.

Most PMMs build 10 things when they should build 3 things really well. Here's the 90-day roadmap prioritized by pipeline impact, not effort.

Weeks 1-3
Lock positioning and messaging
Finalize the story. Get founder, CEO, and sales leader alignment on: "What is SagePilot, in one sentence?" and "Why would a mid-market D2C brand buy?" Run internal positioning workshop. Do not proceed to marketing until this is locked.
Metric: Sales can explain the positioning. Founder says "yes" to all messaging pillars. No more than 2 messaging revisions after this.
Weeks 4-6
Sales enablement, not content
Build 3 things: (1) Discovery deck that tells the story in 8 slides, (2) Battlecards for the top 3 objections, (3) One-page ROI calculator. Get sales to use them in every call. Track what lands.
Metric: Sales uses deck in 10 calls. Battlecard response changes win rate by 3%+. ROI calc sent to 5+ CFOs. Gather feedback.
Weeks 7-9
Homepage and first customer story
Rewrite homepage hero (use the framework above). Publish first case study from a D2C brand showing lifecycle ROI. Create a LinkedIn thread around it. Get this into sales hands to reference during calls.
Metric: Homepage CTR improves 10%+. Case study gets 500+ views. Demo requests from LinkedIn increase 15% week-over-week.
What NOT to do
Don't start a blog series. Don't build vertical hubs. Don't create 10 case studies. Don't launch a newsletter. These all feel productive but they don't move pipeline in the first 90 days. Focus ruthlessly on assets that shorten sales cycle and increase deal velocity. Everything else is phase 2.
